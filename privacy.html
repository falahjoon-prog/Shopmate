Privacy policy

ShopMate AI, crafted by TradeAi · last updated 4 November 2026

ShopMate AI helps a business make invoices and quotes, keep customers, stock and staff records, and estimate painting and tiling jobs. This policy explains what happens to the information you put in it.

Your records stay with you

Customers, invoices, quotes, estimates, stock, staff and photos are stored on your own device. We do not run a server that collects them, and we do not sell or share them.

When information leaves your device (only when you choose)

  • Your Google account: if you connect your Google link, invoices and backups go to your own Google Drive and Gmail.
  • AI features: when you ask the AI a question, or let it read a scope, drawing or invoice, that text or file is sent to the AI service you set up (Google Gemini, with your own key) to get the answer. The on-device AI option runs on the phone and sends nothing.
  • Messages and email: texts and emails to your customers open in your own Messages or email app, and you press Send.
  • Facebook / Instagram: only if you connect them, to post the ads you make.
  • Linked phones: if you link a partner's or staff member's phone, the records you share go to their app through your own Google link.
  • Licence: if you subscribe, your email and licence key are used to check your subscription.

Permissions the app uses

  • Camera and photos: only when you take or pick a photo (receipts, before and after photos, measuring a room). Photos stay on the device.
  • Contacts: the app never reads your contact list. When you add a customer from contacts, the phone's own picker gives the app just the one contact you choose.
  • Notifications and alarms: to remind you about payments, follow-ups, shifts and food-safety tasks.
  • Fingerprint: optional, to unlock the app. Your fingerprint never leaves the phone's secure hardware.

Deleting your information

You can delete any record in the app, or everything with Settings → Reset. Removing the app removes the records on that device. Copies in your own Google Drive stay under your control.

Children

ShopMate AI is a business app and is not made for children.

Contact

Questions about privacy: contact TradeAi through the support details on the download page.
